# Code of conduct

We want CASSL to be a welcoming, harassment-free place to work for everyone, whatever their background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, in reviews especially. Criticise the work, not the person.
- Credit others' work and ideas, in code, data and papers.
- Accept that people make mistakes, and help them fix them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to an owner of the organization, through GitHub. Owners will review every report, keep it
confidential, and may warn, suspend or remove anyone who breaks this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
